How it works

From donation to delivery.

  1. 1
    DonateDrop off a device or arrange a pickup.
  2. 2
    AssessOur team catalogues and tests every device.
  3. 3
    RefurbishData is wiped, hardware repaired, software installed.
  4. 4
    MatchDevices are matched with approved beneficiaries.
  5. 5
    DeliverHandover in-person or shipped with training resources.

Beneficiaries

Who benefits from a device.

Students

Primary, secondary and university students without a personal computer.

Educators

Teachers in under-resourced schools who need tools for lesson planning.

Community programmes

Learning hubs, coding clubs and vocational programmes.
