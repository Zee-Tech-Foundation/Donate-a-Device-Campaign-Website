The campaign

Ten thousand devices. One connected generation.

Our flagship campaign brings donors, partners and beneficiaries together to close the device gap in schools and community learning centres.

Goal

Distribute 10,000 refurbished devices to learners by 2028.

Reach

Prioritise rural schools, girls in STEM, and community learning hubs.

Sustainability

Every device extends its useful life by 3–5 years — reducing e-waste.

How it works

From donation to delivery.

  1. 1
    DonateDrop off a device or arrange a pickup.
  2. 2
    AssessOur team catalogues and tests every device.
  3. 3
    RefurbishData is wiped, hardware repaired, software installed.
  4. 4
    MatchDevices are matched with approved beneficiaries.
  5. 5
    DeliverHandover in-person or shipped with training resources.
