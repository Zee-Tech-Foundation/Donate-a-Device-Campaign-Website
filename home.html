Our mission

Every device is a doorway to opportunity.

We believe learning shouldn't depend on whether a family can afford a laptop. Our mission is simple: no learner left offline.

1

Collect

Individuals and organisations donate laptops, tablets and phones they no longer need.

2

Refurbish

Our technicians wipe, repair and upgrade each device to school-ready condition.

3

Deliver

Devices are placed with vetted students, teachers and community programmes.
