About us

We're a foundation for digital opportunity.

Zee Tech Foundation was founded to make sure that access to technology is never the reason a student falls behind.

Our vision

A world where every learner, everywhere, has the technology they need to reach their full potential.

Our mission

To collect, refurbish and place technology devices with underserved students, teachers and community programmes.

Objectives

What we're working toward.

✓

Distribute 10,000 devices

By 2028, place ten thousand refurbished devices in the hands of learners.

✓

Train 500 educators

Support teachers with digital-literacy workshops in every community we serve.

✓

Divert e-waste

Keep 30 tonnes of electronics out of landfill through refurbishment and recycling.
