What Can You Donate?

We Welcome a Wide Range of Digital Devices

New, gently used, and repairable devices are all welcome.

Computers

Laptops, desktop computers, mini PCs, and all-in-one computers.

Mobile Devices

Tablets and educational tablets suitable for learning.

Accessories

Monitors, keyboards, mice, webcams, chargers, speakers, and headsets.

Storage Devices

SSDs, hard drives, and USB flash drives suitable for reuse.

Networking

Routers, switches, Wi-Fi access points, and networking equipment.

Educational Technology

Projectors, robotics kits, STEM equipment, and learning peripherals.

Not Sure if Your Device Qualifies?

Contact our team before donating. We'll gladly advise whether your device can be refurbished or responsibly recycled.

Your Data Matters

Your Privacy Comes First

Every donated device goes through a secure data sanitization process before refurbishment. Devices that cannot be safely prepared for reuse are responsibly dismantled or recycled.

We are committed to protecting your personal information while giving your device a second life.

From Donation to Impact

Every donated device follows a transparent refurbishment and distribution process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions?

Can I donate one laptop?

Yes. Every device makes a difference.

Can companies donate in bulk?

Absolutely. We welcome donations of any size.

What if my device isn't working?

We assess every device. Many can be repaired or used for parts.

Will my files be deleted?

Yes. Every device undergoes secure data sanitization.

Can I help without donating a device?

Yes. You can volunteer, make a financial contribution, or become a campaign partner.
