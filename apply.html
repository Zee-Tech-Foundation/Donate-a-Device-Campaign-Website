Apply for a Device

Technology Should Never Limit Opportunity.

Need a computer for learning, teaching, work or community impact? Apply for a refurbished device through our Donate a Device – Change a Future Campaign. Every application is reviewed fairly based on need, potential impact and available devices.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

We support people and organisations using technology for education, skills development and community impact.

Students

For school, university, vocational training and online learning.

Teachers

To prepare lessons, teach digitally and access learning resources.

Youth Innovators

Coding, entrepreneurship, robotics and digital skills programmes.

Women & Girls

Supporting education, careers and digital entrepreneurship.

Persons with Disabilities

Improving access to education, communication and employment.

Schools & Organisations

Schools, libraries and nonprofits creating learning opportunities.

Selection

What We Look For

Applications are assessed carefully to ensure every donated device creates the greatest possible impact.

✔ Demonstrated need for a device.
✔ Educational or community purpose.
✔ Potential impact on individuals or communities.
✔ Availability of suitable donated devices.
✔ Fair distribution across communities.

Application Process

How It Works

Every application follows the same transparent process.

1

Apply

Complete the online application form with accurate information.

2

Review

Our team assesses your application based on need and available devices.

3

Verification

Additional information may be requested where necessary.

4

Matching

Successful applicants are matched with suitable donated devices.

5

Notification

Approved applicants receive collection or delivery instructions.

6

Support

Where needed, we provide guidance on safe and effective device use.

Opportunities

How the Device Can Help

Donated devices are intended for education, skills development and community impact.

✔ School assignments and research
✔ Online learning and digital literacy
✔ Coding, AI, STEM and creative work
✔ Job preparation and entrepreneurship
✔ Community education programmes

Fairness

Our Commitment

Every donated device is allocated through a fair and transparent process. We consider genuine need, intended use, potential impact and available inventory to ensure every device creates lasting value.

Recipients Agree To

✔ Use the device for learning, work or community development.
✔ Take reasonable care of the equipment.
✔ Avoid selling or misusing the device.
✔ Participate in follow-up impact assessments if requested.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions You May Have

Here are answers to some common questions about applying for a device.

Is it free to apply?

Yes. Applications are completely free.

Will I definitely receive a device?

No. Devices are allocated based on eligibility, need and availability.

Can schools apply?

Yes. Schools, learning centres and registered organisations are welcome to apply.

Can I apply if I already own a computer?

Yes, if your current device no longer supports your educational or professional needs.

How long does the review take?

Review times vary depending on application volume and available devices.

How will I know the outcome?

Successful applicants will be contacted by email or phone with the next steps.
